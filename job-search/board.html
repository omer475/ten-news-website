Do these first

The next seven days

  1. Fix the graduation date on your CV today.

    It currently reads 2023–2026, which tells every grad-scheme screener you graduated three months ago. That is an automatic rejection for a 2027 scheme.

  2. Apply to the rolling quant and grad programmes before applying anywhere else.

    IMC, SIG, Citadel Securities, Marshall Wace, Man AHL, Revolut and BlackRock all reportedly opened between July and August 2026 and review on a rolling basis. You are roughly four to six weeks into those cycles, not early. Rolling means seats disappear, not that the deadline is soft — and IMC reportedly allows one application per role per year, so do not rush that one.

  3. Send ten cold emails to the AI-in-finance startups.

    Finster AI, Auquan, Rogo, Hebbia, Farsight and Arkera all build what you have already built yourself. Several publish an address and openly invite speculative CVs. This is the single highest-probability route to a part-time October start — far better than any job board.

  4. Apply to Creator Fund.

    It is the one programme found whose eligibility is literally “studying at a UK university and continuing your studies alongside us”. It is built for exactly your constraint, and its alumni go into VC and startups.

  5. Re-run this search with working web access.

    Twelve of the thirty slices got zero coverage, including the bulge-bracket banks, the boutiques, consulting and the job-board sweeps. The boutique banks matter most — they are the likeliest source of an unadvertised off-cycle internship you could start in October.
