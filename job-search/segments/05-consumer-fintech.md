## Intel

- **Revolut is the whole story in this segment right now.** Graduate Programme 2027 applications
  opened May 2026; recruitment runs **July–December 2026**; programme starts split into early
  2027 (Jan–Jun) and **late 2027 (Jul–Dec)** — the late band is the one that fits a summer 2027
  graduation. Selection is **rolling with soft deadlines**: reporting consistently says
  Data Science and Strategy quotas fill by **mid-to-late October** even though the portal stays
  open to December. **Treat early October 2026 as the real deadline.**
- Revolut runs a genuine internal pipeline: **top-performing interns are offered a Graduate
  Programme place the following year.** He can't use that route (missed the penultimate-year
  internship), so the direct grad application is his only door — one more reason to be early.
- Revolut's UK grad roles are also open to relocation candidates (PL/PT/ES/UAE/UK) with visa
  support — meaning **a large international applicant pool**. His ILR is not a differentiator
  here the way it is at banks; his differentiator has to be the work.
- Revolut warns publicly that it **only recruits through official revolut.com channels** and uses
  no third-party recruiters — ignore any Revolut "recruiter" approach that arrives off-platform.
- **Monzo genuinely has no graduate scheme** — its careers FAQ says so explicitly. Anyone telling
  him to "apply to the Monzo grad scheme" is wrong. The real route is the **"Associate ..."**
  job titles, posted ad hoc on Greenhouse. Monzo also hires **rolling** and fills cohorts, so
  its internship reqs (when they appear, historically **October/November** with a **mid-November**
  close — Summer 2026 SWE closed 14 Nov 2025) reward week-one applications.
- Monzo intern pay benchmark: **£40,000 pro rata**, London, hybrid, 12-week minimum. Useful
  anchor when negotiating elsewhere in the segment.
- **Wise posts each early-careers stream as its own vacancy with its own closing date** — there
  is no single firm-wide deadline, and vacancies are pulled once enough candidates reach final
  interview. The 2027 cycle was expected to open **September–October 2026**, so `wise.jobs/early-careers`
  should be checked weekly *right now*. Note also that Wise's **Analytics Intern** spec accepted
  **penultimate OR final-year** students (graduating 2026 *or* 2027) — Wise is unusually
  final-year-friendly, so when the 2027 Analytics Intern posting appears it is worth reading
  the eligibility line rather than assuming he's excluded.
- Wise runs **two grad cohorts** (there was an explicit "September 2026 … Autumn cohort"),
  which means more than one entry point per year.
- **Trackers worth wiring up** rather than manual checking: The Trackr
  (https://app.the-trackr.com/company/starling-bank — was the Bristol Tracker, so it is
  Bristol-student oriented and already lists Starling's TBC 2027 dates) and The Intake's UK
  grad/internship tracker (https://www.jointheintake.com/tracker). Also
  https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships, updated daily, which covers
  data/AI/quant internships including UK postings.

  unfetchable from this session but fine from a browser): Monzo → Greenhouse
  (`job-boards.greenhouse.io/monzo`); Tide → Greenhouse (`job-boards.greenhouse.io/tide`);
