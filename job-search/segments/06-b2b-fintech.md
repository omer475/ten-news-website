## Intel

1. **Structural finding for Track A: this segment is a poor hunting ground for part-time term-time internships.** Every early-careers programme that surfaced across Stripe, Checkout.com, Adyen, Thought Machine, Airwallex and Worldpay is a **fixed-window, full-time, cohort-based** programme (8–12 weeks summer, or a 6-month Jan–Jun placement). None advertise 1–3 days/week. The realistic Track A route into B2B fintech is therefore **speculative email to the smaller risk-AI / open-banking companies** (Sardine, Napier AI, Ravelin, Griffin, Yapily, Volt, Token.io), not applications to advertised programmes.
2. **Penultimate-year gate is explicit and is the main Track B hazard.** Checkout.com states internships are for penultimate-year students and graduate roles for those who have completed their degree; Thought Machine's internship gives preference to 2027 graduates (i.e. penultimate-years in the 2026 cycle). For a summer-2027 graduate, **the grad programme is the only door** at these firms — do not spend time on their summer-2027 internships.
3. **Timing pattern (from secondary sources, unconfirmed — verify on the employer's own page):** Checkout.com grad applications described as opening **September 2026** with a soft deadline around **Jan/Feb 2027**; Adyen EMEA described as opening **Sept–Oct 2026** for an Aug/Sept-2027 start; Stripe university applications described as opening **early August 2026** and filling on a rolling basis by **mid-November 2026**. If these hold, **Stripe and Checkout.com are live right now and Stripe is the most time-critical** — rolling and already a month into its window.
4. **Rolling-basis warning.** Stripe and Adyen were both described as rolling with acceptance rates falling sharply through the cycle (Adyen: ~5% early vs ~2% late). For rolling schemes, submitting in September/October is worth more than a stronger application in January.
5. **Worldpay runs a dedicated graduate microsite** (`graduates.worldpay.com/schemes`) separate from its main job board — a genuinely structured 2-year rotational scheme, and the most conventional "grad scheme" in this segment. Worldpay also now owns Ravelin, so an ML-fraud interest is a coherent story to tell them.
6. **ATS map for when fetching works again** (all confirmed by URL, useful for the re-run): TrueLayer → Greenhouse *and* Workable; Ravelin → Pinpoint *and* Workable; Napier AI → Pinpoint; Sardine → Ashby; Paddle → Consider; Airwallex → own ATS at `careers.airwallex.com` with stable per-job UUID URLs; Worldpay/Global Payments → `jobs.worldpay.com` / `jobs.globalpayments.com`.
