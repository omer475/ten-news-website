## Intel

**Verified this session:** nothing. The items below are prior knowledge (model knowledge, cutoff May 2026), included because the orchestrator will otherwise have no scaffold for the re-run. Each must be confirmed before it drives a schedule.

- **Structural caveat that may reshape this whole segment.** At bulge-bracket banks the September full-time analyst class is overwhelmingly filled from the previous year's summer internship — commonly quoted as 80–95% conversion. Direct-entry full-time analyst applications for a candidate who did *not* intern at the bank are, at several of these firms, either not offered at all or a very thin residual process. The orchestrator should not assume 29 open front-door 2027 FT programmes exist. The realistic Track B pipeline here may be far narrower than the segment brief implies, and the re-run should explicitly record, per bank, *whether a direct FT route exists at all* — not just its deadline.
- **Because of that, the highest-value thing to check per bank is which of three states it is in:** (a) open 2027 direct-entry FT analyst application; (b) FT hiring is internship-conversion only, so the accessible route is an off-cycle internship (Track A) or a 2027 summer internship if final-years are eligible; (c) nothing. My prior expectation is that (b) is the most common state.
- **Rolling review is the norm** where these programmes exist — applications assessed as received and closed early once the class fills, so a stated deadline is an upper bound, not the real one. This makes the missing verification more costly than usual: the cost of a week's delay is real. Prioritise re-running this segment.
- **Final-year eligibility for 2027 summer internships** (brief's note under Track B) is worth checking bank by bank rather than assuming exclusion — a few large banks do accept finalists into summer programmes with a deferred/graduate-entry framing. Unverified.
- **Off-cycle internships (Track A)** are more common at the European and Japanese houses in this list than at the US bulge brackets, and are frequently 3–6 months full-time rather than part-time — so under the brief's Track A rule they would need flagging as full-time and are likely incompatible with final-year study. Unverified.
- **Route around the current blocker:** the banks' own sites, all major ATS hosts and all UK graduate aggregators are blocked by egress policy, so a re-run needs either restored egress or a WebSearch budget. If only search is restored, snippets alone will give programme existence and often the deadline, which is most of the value here.
