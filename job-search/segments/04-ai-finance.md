## Intel

1. **Fetch was impossible this session.** WebFetch returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` for *every* domain including greenhouse.io, ashbyhq.com, and even wikipedia.org — this is an environment-wide policy block, not a per-site anti-bot measure. The BRIEF's assumption that ATS boards are fetchable did not hold here. Any re-run needs egress to `job-boards.greenhouse.io`, `boards-api.greenhouse.io`, `jobs.ashbyhq.com`, `apply.workable.com`, `ats.rippling.com`, `join.com` and `*.myworkdayjobs.com` at minimum. `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/<token>/jobs` returns clean JSON and is the highest-yield endpoint if it can be unblocked (tokens confirmed by search: `alphasense`, `hebbia`, `boostedai`, `similarweb`).
2. **The whole segment's hiring pattern is inbound email, not posted reqs.** Three of these companies publish a hiring email address outright — **careers@finster.ai**, **hello@auquan.com**, **jobs@arkera.ai** — and Finster's careers page literally says to write in when nothing is posted. For a 29-person and an 11-50-person London startup, a well-targeted email with a working multi-agent trading system attached is a far higher-probability route than any application form in this file.
3. **Off-cycle intake calendar:** a search snippet stated off-cycle internships are recruited **quarterly with start dates in Q1 January, Q2 April, Q3 July and Q4 October**. So an October 2026 start is a standard intake, and applications for it are normally in by mid-summer — meaning the Q4-2026 window may already be closing and **Q1-2027 (January) is the realistic off-cycle target**. Worth setting expectations with Omer on this.
4. **ATS map for the segment** (useful for a re-run): Greenhouse → AlphaSense, Hebbia, Boosted.ai, Similarweb. Ashby → Rogo, Fintool. Workable → Quantexa. Rippling → Daloopa. join.com → Cognitive Credit, Tenity. FactorialHR → Cognitive Credit. Comeet → Bridgewise. Workday → Kensho/S&P Global.

MDOTM — **Tommaso Migliore** and **Federico Mazzorin** (co-founders, founded in London). These are small enough companies that founder outreach is appropriate.
