## Intel
- **Structural point for the orchestrator (this is the key finding for this segment):** the BRIEF's Track B premise mostly does **not** hold in mega-cap PE. These firms overwhelmingly fill full-time analyst seats from their own **summer analyst class** (recruited in penultimate year) or by hiring **2–3-year investment-banking analysts**. A "September 2027 graduate scheme, applications open autumn 2026" of the kind that exists at banks and consultancies is rare here. The realistic mega-cap PE routes for a summer-2027 graduate are (a) the small number of true direct-from-university analyst programmes, and (b) off-cycle internships converting to full-time.
- **Direct-from-university programmes** — my remit named Blackstone, Apollo, KKR, EQT and Hg as the firms that recruit analysts straight from university rather than only from banks. I was able to corroborate London/EMEA campus activity for **Blackstone, KKR and Apollo** only; **EQT and Hg went unsearched**.
- **Blackstone UK campus timing:** search results indicated the UK 2027 Summer Analyst window runs **15 June – 30 October 2026 on a rolling basis**, applications submitted through **Blackstone Campus Careers on Workday**, with **separate listings per investment group** (Private Equity, Real Estate, Credit, Hedge Fund Solutions). Candidates may apply to multiple groups but should tailor the motivation per group. Process: first-round video interview, then superday-style rounds. **UNVERIFIED** — I could not open the Workday board. If any of these London listings turns out to accept final-years, the late-October rolling deadline makes it the most time-sensitive item in this segment, so **checking Blackstone's eligibility wording is the single highest-priority follow-up.**
- **KKR selectivity, for expectation-setting:** search results reported ~30–50 summer analysts taken globally (NY, Menlo Park, London, Hong Kong) from ~7,000 applications — sub-1% acceptance.
- **Apollo** confirmed to run summer programmes at **both Analyst and Associate level in London**, with a reported 75–85% intern-to-full-time conversion rate. High conversion means the internship, not the graduate door, is the real entry point.
