## Intel

- **Deadline pattern for crypto market-makers:** the grad/intern cycle runs *early*. Wintermute's
  Graduate Quant Researcher for a **Summer 2026** start closed **10 March 2026** — i.e. ~5 months
  before start, applications open the preceding autumn. So the **2027-start Wintermute grad req
  should open on the Lever board during autumn 2026 — right now**. This is the single most
  actionable piece of timing intel in the segment: set a weekly check on
  `jobs.lever.co/wintermute-trading`.
- **Coinbase 2027–28 intern applications reportedly open rolling from Sept/Oct 2026**, and Coinbase's
  intern programme is described as open to "students, non-students, and graduates regardless of age"
  — worth testing, since most summer-2027 programmes exclude final-years.
- **Copper.co runs a fixed annual cycle**: applications close ~11 May, interviews the following two
  weeks, 8-week summer block. So the 2027 equivalent will close around **May 2027**.
- **B2C2's internship is a 10-week July block**, with an explicit conversion-to-permanent path, and
  the graduate programme lets you "assume trading responsibility within the first year" — unusually
  fast for a grad seat, and a good line to quote back in a cover letter.
- **Keyrock's intern req appears to have been reposted** (two distinct totaljobs IDs for the same
  role). Reposting usually means either a live, hard-to-fill req or a rolling pipeline — either way
  it is worth applying even if the posting date looks old.
- **Structural note on this segment:** crypto firms overwhelmingly run *full-time* summer blocks
  (8–12 weeks) or *full-time* 6-month off-cycles. Genuine 1–3 day/week term-time internships are
  close to non-existent here. For Track A, the realistic routes are (a) the Keyrock 6-month
  off-cycle, taken full-time, or (b) speculative emails to the small London shops — Nickel Digital,
  Archax, Zodia Markets — where headcount is small enough that a bespoke part-time research seat can
  be created off an inbound email. Route (b) is where his 17-agent trading system does the most work.
- **Low-friction application boards** (accept general/speculative applications): Zodia Custody on
  Workable (`apply.workable.com/zodia-custody/`), Archax on its own ATS (`careers.archax.com/jobs`).
- **His ILR is a genuine edge here**: Zodia (Standard Chartered-backed) and LMAX Digital sit inside
  bank/regulated structures that routinely decline sponsorship. Lead with "no sponsorship required".
