## Intel

- **Rolling is the rule, not the exception.** SIG, Citadel Securities, Jane Street, Jump and IMC
  were all described as rolling with no posted closing date. The operative deadline is seat
  exhaustion, not a date. Citadel Securities' cycle is reported to have **most offers signed by
  mid-autumn** — so early-to-mid October 2026 is the practical wall for this segment.
- **Cycle opening dates actually seen:** IMC 2027 reqs went up **July 2026**; Citadel Securities'
  university-grad req **24 July 2026**; Jane Street London Summer 2027 **~3 Aug 2026**.
  Everything is already open — he is 4–6 weeks late to the start of the cycle, not early.
- **IMC's one-application-per-role-per-year rule** is the single most important application-hygiene
  fact in this segment. A rushed IMC application costs him the whole 2027 cycle there.
- **The "graduating Sept 2026 – July 2027" eligibility window on the IMC grad trader req** is the
  clearest confirmation I found that final-year 2027 grads are in scope for a Track B role.
  Use that phrasing to screen other firms' reqs fast.
- **Degree-bar pattern:** Quantitative *Researcher* reqs across Citadel Securities, G-Research and
  SIG sit behind PhD/Masters channels. Quantitative *Trader* reqs (IMC, SIG systematic, Optiver,
  Jane Street) accept broader backgrounds and explicitly train from scratch. **Point him at trader
  tracks, not researcher tracks.**
- **ILR is worth nothing here as a differentiator** — these firms sponsor freely and compete for
  visa-holders. It removes a barrier; it is not a selling point. Do not lead with it.
- **Fetchability note for whoever re-runs this:** `careers.sig.com`, `imc.com`,
  `citadelsecurities.com`, `janestreet.com` and `gresearch.com` all publish real, indexable job
  pages and should be fetchable from an unrestricted environment. Aggregators that surfaced
  accurate, current London quant reqs in search: **tradermath.org**, **openquant.co**,
  **quantblueprint.com**, **prosple.com**. Avoid LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor.
